# SafeChat Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Last updated:** 2026-11-05

This is a **draft** privacy notice for product development. Have counsel review before production.

## 1. Data we collect
- Account data: username, display name, optional email/phone, profile photo, bio
- Membership: Pro or Premium plan and related settings
- Messages, status updates, call metadata (not always call media)
- Device and technical data: IP, user agent, session tokens
- Usage and diagnostics as needed to secure the service

## 2. How we use data
- Provide messaging, status, groups, and calling
- Authenticate sessions and prevent abuse
- Apply Pro/Premium entitlements
- Send transactional notices (e.g. deactivation reminders)

## 3. Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We may share with processors (hosting, email) under contracts, or when required by law.

## 4. Retention
- Active accounts: while the account remains open
- Deactivated accounts: retained up to about 6 months before possible permanent deletion, unless reactivated earlier
- Backups and logs may persist for a limited security window

## 5. Your choices
- Edit profile and privacy settings in-app
- Deactivate or request permanent deletion
- Control who sees last seen, photo, status, and read receipts (subject to Premium rules where applicable)

## 6. Security
We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, hashed passwords, session controls). No method is 100% secure.

## 7. Children
SafeChat is not directed at children under the age required by local law.

## 8. International transfers
Data may be processed in regions where we or our providers operate.

## 9. Contact
Use in-app Support for privacy requests (access, correction, deletion).
