# SafeChat User Agreement (Draft)

**Last updated:** 2026-11-05

This is a **draft** agreement for product development. Have counsel review before production.

## 1. Acceptance
By creating an account or using SafeChat, you agree to this User Agreement and our Privacy Policy.

## 2. Accounts
- You must provide accurate registration information.
- You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials.
- Default membership is **Pro** (free tier). **Premium** is optional and may be paid.

## 3. Acceptable use
You agree not to harass, spam, distribute illegal content, reverse engineer the service abusively, or attempt unauthorized access.

## 4. Content
You retain rights to content you post. You grant SafeChat a limited license to host, transmit, and display that content to operate the service (messaging, status, calls).

## 5. Premium features
Premium features (including but not limited to live typing, forced read-receipt visibility, verification badge, and status advertisements) are subject to eligibility, fair use, and future payment terms.

## 6. Deactivation and deletion
You may **deactivate** your account. After approximately **six (6) months** without reactivation, we may permanently delete account data. We may send periodic email reminders while deactivated. You may request earlier permanent deletion.

## 7. Termination
We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this agreement or applicable law.

## 8. Disclaimers
SafeChat is provided “as is.” Real-time calling and delivery depend on network conditions and device permissions.

## 9. Contact
Support channels published in-app apply for questions about this agreement.
